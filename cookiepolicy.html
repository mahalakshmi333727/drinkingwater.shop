Cookie Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

In short

drinkingwater.shop does not set any cookies of its own. It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and it does not run a visitor-tracking or profiling service. The site stores two small display preferences in your browser’s local storage so that it can remember your choices.

What the site stores on your device

When you use the theme or sound controls, your choice is saved in your browser’s local storage. This data stays on your device and is not sent to our server.

NamePurposeType
dws-themeRemembers whether you chose the deep or lab light colour theme.Local storage
dws-soundRemembers whether ambient sound is switched on or off.Local storage

These entries stay until you clear your browser’s site data. The site works without them; it simply falls back to its default settings.

Resources loaded from other services

To display fonts and run the interactive 3D experience, pages load files from these services:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) for typefaces.
  • cdnjs (cdnjs.cloudflare.com) for the GSAP animation library.
  • jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) for the Three.js 3D library.

Whenever a browser requests a file from a service like these, that service receives technical information such as your IP address and browser details. We do not use these services for analytics or advertising, and we do not control how they handle the requests they receive; please see their own privacy policies for details.

Contact form and server logs

The contact forms on the homepage send the information you submit to Web3Forms for delivery to info@drinkingwater.shop. The site itself does not store your form submission. Like most websites, the hosting provider may also keep standard server logs (such as IP address, date and requested page) for security and operation.

Managing or removing stored data

You can delete the stored preferences at any time by clearing site data for drinkingwater.shop in your browser settings. You can also block cookies and site storage in your browser; the site will keep working.

Changes to this policy

If the site starts using analytics, advertising or other cookies, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
